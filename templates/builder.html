Getting Started

  1. Sign in with Patreon. Open the {{module.label}} tab and click Sign in with Patreon. A browser window opens; approve it and come back. Signing in is what gives you your monthly generation allowance.
  2. Pick a quick template, or write your own description. The Quick template… dropdown above the description box fills in a worked example you can edit. Detail pays off: mention role, temperament, equipment, and how you intend to use the {{documentNoun}} at the table.
  3. Set the mechanical fields. Name and level (or CR) steer the stat block. Leave a field blank and the generator picks something sensible for you.
  4. Click Generate (or press Ctrl+Enter). The badge on the button shows what it costs against your monthly allowance. Your {{documentNoun}} is created in Foundry and its sheet opens.
  5. Tweak and reuse. Every generation is logged in the history panel on the right. Click an entry to load its settings back into the form, then change one detail and go again.

Failed generations are not charged. If something breaks, the history entry keeps a Retry button, and the Send Feedback box at the bottom of the window reports it to us with the error attached.

Getting more out of the builder

  • Suggest a name. The dice button beside the Name field invents a thematic name from your description, and costs nothing.
  • Undo Last Generation. The button under the form deletes the most recent creation if it came out wrong. It does not refund the use.
  • Filter the history. The search box above the history list matches on name, and the button collapses the panel when you want more room for the form.
  • Keyboard. Ctrl+Enter generates, Esc clears the form.
  • Portraits from a sheet. Any actor sheet gets a Generate Image button, so you can add art to something you made earlier without regenerating it.
  • Art style. Set one below and every portrait follows it.
